Design for Precision Engineering

The Design for Precision Engineering (DPE) research line is part of the Control Systems Technology (CST) group within the Department of Mechanical Engineering at TU/e. It draws on the rich heritage of the Dutch school of Design Principles and focuses on the design and realization of state-of-the-art precision hardware.

About DPE

The DPE research line has a long-lasting tradition of application-driven research in medical robotics, scientific instrumentation, and a wide variety of industrial applications — most prominently semiconductor equipment. This emphasis on real-world application has produced hardware solutions adopted in major scientific projects and high-tech industries alike, and has laid the foundation for several successful start-ups and scale-ups.

The lab combines fundamental precision engineering knowledge with a strong focus on hardware realization. Research problems are selected based on their relevance to industry, and solutions are validated through physical prototypes and experiments.

Focus Areas

DPE bundles three focus areas that all share a commitment to the Dutch tradition of precision design and the realization of state-of-the-art hardware.

Mechanical Design

Focused on the design of precision mechanical structures and mechanisms. Core topics include exact constraint design, flexure-based mechanisms, structural dynamics, and the design of ultra-precise positioning systems. The goal is hardware that is predictable, reproducible, and manufacturable.

Learn more ↗

Mechatronic Systems Design

Addresses the design of complete mechatronic systems in which mechanical design, actuation, sensing and control are developed in concert. Research includes precision motion stages, active vibration isolation, piezoelectric actuation, and the integration of closed-loop control with high-performance mechanical hardware. This is the area where my own research is primarily situated.

Learn more ↗

Opto-Mechatronics

Focuses on the interplay between optics and precision mechanics in high-end systems. Key application areas include lithography, metrology, and scientific instrumentation where optical performance is directly dependent on the quality of the underlying mechanical and mechatronic design.

Application Domains

DPE works across a range of high-tech application domains where precision, reliability, and performance are non-negotiable. Our research partners include leading companies and institutes in:

Semiconductor Equipment

Lithography systems, electron beam inspection, wafer handling — where nanometer-level accuracy and high throughput must coexist.

Scientific Instrumentation

Electron microscopes, synchrotron end-stations, and other large-scale scientific instruments demanding extreme stability and precision.

Medical Technology

Surgical robots and medical imaging equipment where precision design intersects with stringent safety and reliability requirements.
